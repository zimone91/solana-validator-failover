# Audits 1–2 — v0.6.8 (takeover speed-up), June 2026

**Scope.** Audit 1 reviewed the *design* of the v0.6.8 takeover speed-up (bounded demote with
hard-stop escalation; flap hysteresis for the egress-only self-fence; the optional gossip
identity-flip fast-path). Audit 2 reviewed the *implementation* diff.

**Audit 1 (design) — key outcomes.**
- The gossip fast-path was constrained to a **positive proof shape** (the holder's known *unstaked*
  pubkey appearing at the staked identity's own gossip endpoint, corroborated on two vantages,
  after a prior-absent edge) and shipped **off by default**, fail-closed on any missing knob.
- The demote path gained hard time bounds (`timeout -k`) and an escalation ladder ending in a
  verified stop — a self-fence is only as good as its demote actually completing.

**Audit 2 (implementation) — 6 fixes before tag (internally S1–S6).**
1. The fast-path stagger floor was computed but not *enforced* for a second spare — enforced.
2. A failed takeover incorrectly reset the fast-path episode, letting a stale observation satisfy
   the next episode's absent→present edge — reset discipline fixed.
3. Admin-socket calls lacked the `-k` kill-escalation on timeout — added everywhere.
4. The hard-stop reported success without re-verifying the validator was actually down — it now
   verifies, and (in v0.6.9) re-verifies after a delay to catch a `Restart=always` resurrection.
5–6. Two test suites asserted the presence of code rather than its behavior — rewritten as
   behavioral tests with revert-controls ("de-vacuuming"), which became house style.

**Direction check.** No double-sign vector was found in either round; all findings failed toward
availability loss or missing enforcement of an intended bound.
